How to mark a tray
  • Outer edge: click points on the tray's outside edge, at least 2 on each side, away from the rounded corners. If meat covers part of an edge, click where you can see it.
  • Lip inner edge: switch mode and click where the flat lip ends and the wall drops down, on any side where you can see it. Skip sides hidden by meat.
  • Save, then look at the comparison. It shows how far the model's lines are from yours.

Keys: click adds a point, Z undo, Tab switch mode, Enter save, ← → move.
